A MULTI-POLICY APPROACH BASED ON CLUSTERING FOR MINIMIZING THE DAMAGE ON A FALLING BALLBOT

G. Buzzetti, M. Aractingi, D. Zappetti, G. Iacca, CoDIT 2025. DOI:10.1109/CoDIT66093.2025.11321766

This work introduces a multi-policy approach combining clustering and reinforcement learning to minimize damage during falls in humanoid ballbot robots.

A REINFORCEMENT LEARNING METHOD TO MINIMIZE THE DAMAGE ON A FALLING BALLBOT

G. Buzzetti, D. Zappetti, G. Iacca, ERF 2025. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-031-76424-0_15

This work investigates the use of deep reinforcement learning to reduce fall damage in Miroki, a humanoid ballbot robot. We develop an RL framework in Isaac Gym to train a control policy that uses the robot's arms to mitigate impacts during falls. Our results show a 52% reduction in fall damage compared to uncontrolled falls.

REINFORCEMENT LEARNING FRAMEWORK FOR AUTONOMOUS DRIVING WITH CARMAKER

https://github.com/giu950/CarmakerAI

Technologies: Python · Reinforcement Learning · PPO · IPG CarMaker · Gym · Stable-Baselines3

This project provides a Python framework for training and evaluating reinforcement learning agents in the IPG CarMaker simulator. It integrates CarMaker with Gym and Stable-Baselines3, allowing users to define custom environments, reward functions, and control strategies.
